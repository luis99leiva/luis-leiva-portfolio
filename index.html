Case study

Taking a fintech app fully digital

Opening any financial product at Tu Financiera meant coordinating with an advisor over WhatsApp, and users couldn’t even see the products they owned.

In a team of three, I owned the core experience: automated sign-up, self-service product requests, and a design system built from scratch.

The new app launched with an NPS of +73.5.
